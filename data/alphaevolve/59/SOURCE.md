# AlphaEvolve problem 59: record sets

`record_64.json` (112 points) and `record_100.json` (164 points) are the best-known isosceles-free subsets of the
64x64 and 100x100 grids. They were extracted unchanged from the notebook
`experiments/subsets_of_the_grid_with_no_isosceles_triangles/` of the repository
google-deepmind/alphaevolve_repository_of_problems (clone dated 2026-07-11) and are credited to AlphaEvolve
(Georgiev, Gomez-Serrano, Tao, Wagner, arXiv 2511.02864, section 6.39). Coordinates are 0..n-1.
Both pass `ante.problems.iso.exact.check`.
